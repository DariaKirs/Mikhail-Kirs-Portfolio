Local Business Storytelling · Video Series

Forest City Spotlight

A social video series highlighting local places, businesses and experiences in London, Ontario. The production approach combines story structure, location planning, iPhone filming, editing, subtitles, graphics, music and on-screen text.

RoleVideo Content Producer
FormatLocal business social video
LocationLondon, Ontario
CaptureiPhone

The idea

Show the character of a local place, not just its address.

Each episode is designed around the people, atmosphere and details that make a business memorable to a local audience.

Episodes

Local stories shaped for social media.

Business names, real covers and exact publication links will be inserted after the final episode materials are selected.
