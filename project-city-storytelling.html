London · Winter 2024 · Two-Part Film

A city told through a personal journey.

Filmed during a winter trip to London, this two-part personal project uses the journey itself as a structure for exploring the city — its neighbourhoods, architecture, history, transport, street culture and everyday life.

Part I

Neighbourhoods, architecture and the route into London

From Gants Hill and Islington to Shoreditch, London Bridge and St Paul’s, the first part follows the route into and through the city, combining neighbourhood observations, public transport, architecture, landmarks and the A Wild Life for Wildlife exhibition.

Watch Part I on YouTube ↗

Part II

Landmarks, street life and the atmosphere of the city

The second part moves through Westminster, Trafalgar Square, Soho, Hyde Park, Buckingham Palace and the City, mixing historical context with theatre, street musicians, food, shops, humour and everyday moments from the trip.
