About

Mikhail Kirs

Mikhail is a Video Content Producer and Interactive Media Design graduate based in London, Ontario. His work spans Personal Brand Content, Local Business Storytelling, long-form city narratives and visual communication.

He combines planning, filming and post-production in one workflow: refining ideas, developing scripts, selecting locations, organizing the shoot, filming on iPhone, editing the complete video, and adding subtitles, historical inserts, music, graphics and on-screen text.
