Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

This website collects only the information needed to respond when you choose to contact Mikhail Kirs.

Information collected

When you use the contact form, the information collected is your name, email address and the message you send. Basic technical request information may also be processed by the website’s hosting and security infrastructure when the form is submitted.

How the information is used

The information is used only to review your inquiry, respond to you, maintain relevant correspondence, and protect the form from spam or misuse.

Contact-form information is not sold. It is not used for marketing emails or newsletters unless you separately choose to give consent for that purpose in the future.

Services involved

  • Vercel hosts the website and runs the server-side form and email-processing endpoints.
  • Resend provides email delivery and, for email sent directly to the public contact address, email receiving infrastructure.

These providers process information only as needed to provide the technical services used by this website.

How long messages are kept

Contact messages and related correspondence are normally kept for up to 24 months after the last meaningful exchange. They may be kept longer when an active project, administrative need, legal obligation, or security issue reasonably requires it, and may be deleted earlier when no longer needed.

Access, correction and deletion

You may ask what contact information is being held about you, request a correction, or ask for deletion where reasonably possible. A request may require enough information to identify the relevant correspondence.

Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to limit unnecessary collection and protect the contact process. No online transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Privacy questions or requests

Email contact@mikhailkirs.com.
