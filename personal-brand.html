Personal Brand Content

A visual brand is not a mask.

It is a visual interpretation of the public position you choose to present.

What do you want to bring into public view?

What comes into view

Expertise

What you know. What you do. What you are genuinely good at.

Contribution

What your work, knowledge or ideas can give to other people.

Purpose

Why this work matters and what you want it to achieve.

Principles

The standards and relevant beliefs that guide the public position you choose to present.

Evidence

The work, actions, projects and results that make that public position credible.

You choose the boundary.

Personal Brand Content does not require turning private life into content. We work with the part you choose to make public.

The visual context is part of the message.

Location, people, activity, framing, and visual context are not decoration around the message but part of the communication itself.
